What the numbers actually mean

On a single-zero European wheel every pocket has the same 1-in-37 chance on each spin. Over a short window some numbers come up more often ("hot") and some rarely or not at all ("cold"). That is normal randomness, not a wheel leaning one way - the wheel has no memory. Tracking frequencies and streaks just helps you read the table at a glance instead of relying on memory.
